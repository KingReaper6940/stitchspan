    THE COUNT RULES

    The technique changes the arithmetic.

    M1 · zero in, one out

    Add between distinct original stitches. Flat plans reserve at least one plain stitch in every gap, keeping insertions inside the working area. A round may insert at its seam.

    KFB · one in, two out

    Each change works an existing source stitch twice. Those stitches are subtracted before the remaining plain stitches are distributed.

    K2tog · two in, one out

    Each change consumes a nonoverlapping pair of original stitches. The plan cannot decrease by more than the available pairs.

    The audit checks exact counts, coverage, protected edges, distinct M1 gaps and plain-gap balance. It does not check gauge, fit, shaping aesthetics, mirror symmetry, stitch patterns or fabric behavior. Full rules and limitations.
